04 / HOW I WORK

My design process.

Every project is an opportunity to understand, explore, and create with purpose.

01

Discover

I start by learning about your business, your audience, your goals, and what you want your website or product to achieve.

02

Explore

I explore ideas, review existing experiences, and think through the structure and content before moving into visual design.

03

Design

I translate ideas into wireframes and visual designs, focusing on clarity, usability, and a visual identity that feels right for your brand.

04

Refine

I review the design, gather feedback, and make adjustments to create an experience that feels clear and intuitive.
